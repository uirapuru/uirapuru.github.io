Kurs Strzelców Wyborowych / Sharpshooter Academy

Version 1.1.0 · 13 November 2026

Android app

The 12 Shots course on a phone, in Polish or English: 12 chapters, 159 narrated and subtitled videos, a test after every chapter. Videos can be downloaded and watched offline.

Get it on Google Play

Why it exists

The 12 Shots course runs on strzelecwyborowy.pl, in a browser, after signing in. At the range or in the field it is handier on a phone, with no account and no network.

The app has the same videos as the course on the website. Purchased chapters belong to your Google account, so they come back on a new phone.

How it works

  • Chapter 1 is free. Chapters 2 to 12 are bought separately in the app, through Google Play.
  • Chapters unlock in order. The next one opens once you have watched the videos of the previous one and passed its test.
  • A test has 10 random questions. Passing takes 8 correct answers. Attempts are unlimited.
  • Every chapter has a printable shooting card, and some chapters also have targets.

Offline

Videos stream from a server. You can also download a whole chapter to the phone. Downloaded videos are encrypted and play without a network. Menus, tests and statistics work offline.

Two languages

Since version 1.1.0 the app also has the English edition of the course, Sharpshooter Academy. You pick the language on first launch and change it under „About”. A purchased chapter opens in both languages.

Privacy

The app has no account, no login, no adverts and no analytics. It sends the server only a Google Play purchase token, a chapter number and the course language, to release the videos of a purchased chapter.
