Privacy policy

What this site collects and what it does not.

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no forms, no visitor counters and no adverts. The site sets no cookies.

What the server collects

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs. I have no access to them. GitHub's terms are set out in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Files on Google Drive

The download buttons point to files on Google Drive. Downloading happens on Google's terms and under its privacy policy. I cannot see who downloaded a file.

Apps

The apps have their own privacy policies, because they collect data the site does not:

  • TakLab Hub — privacy policy (Polish)
  • TakLab Hub — account and data deletion (Polish)
  • SniperTAK — privacy policy

Data enquiries
