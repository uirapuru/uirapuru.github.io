Tactical Networking Field Handbook

Polish translation · 127 pages · PDF

A translation of the field handbook on IP networking: addressing and NAT, MANET mesh radios, satellite, LTE and 5G links, PACE planning and IP camera video into ATAK. 127 pages.

Download PDF

What it covers

Part one covers the basics: how packets travel, how to split addresses into subnets, and what NAT, routing, DNS, DHCP, VLANs, firewalls, VPN tunnels and QoS do.

Part two covers bearers: MANET mesh radios, low-orbit satellite terminals, LTE and 5G. It also shows how to build them into a PACE plan in which the network fails over to a backup link on its own.

Part three joins the networks of several units into one mission network. Part four takes video from an IP camera across the radio network into ATAK.

Every chapter ends with field pitfalls and a way to check that the network works. Port, subnet and glossary references close the book.

Where it came from

This is a Polish translation made for private use. The original was published by The Communicator LLC in 2026. Copyright in the text belongs to the original authors.

Who it is for

People who set up communications for a team working with ATAK: TAK server, mesh radios, satellite terminal and cameras. No prior networking knowledge is assumed.

Language

The translation is in Polish.
